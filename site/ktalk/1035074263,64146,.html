{ DE_Inferno Updated }
posted Saturday, October 19, 2002 by Some1 Call 911
Narby tells CS-Nation that like DE_Aztec he has done a whole load of changes on DE_Inferno for CS version 1.6. The only major thing he didn't change was the gameplay (thank God). Here's a look at it:



Here's the change log that Narby sent to CS-N:
  • Updated texture set
  • Added various ambient details
  • Fixed bug that allowed players to throw flashbangs over the roofs
  • Fixed floaty crates
  • Fixed broken light patches
  • Brush tweaks
  • New skybox
  • Added extra clipping
    • I think I'm going to like Inferno a lot more than I did before. It was already one of my favourite DE_ maps gameplay wise but now it looks sexier than before so it may be my favourite. For more information and the rest of the screenshots head over to CS-Nation.
