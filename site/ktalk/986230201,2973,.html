{ CS-Radio and MOD MAG mapping contest }
posted Monday, April 2, 2001 by ThatHurt[CSR]
We are happy to announce the following news

MOD Magazine and CS Radio are proud to present a CS Radio Mapping contest,
Starting April 1, 2001 MOD Magazine and CS Radio will be taking in contest
entries at contest@mod-mag.com.Which will include a map with the CS Radio theme, and it must have CS Radio and MOD Mag somewhere in the map and has to be visible for everyone else to see, the map must also be original if it is not you will
be disqualified from the contest and not aloud to enter a second time.
There is not limit on how many times you can enter the contest, unless you
have been disqualified. The contest will end April 30, 2001 and the
winner will be announced May 5, 2001 the winner will receive a copy of
Half-Life: Platinum and get there map in the MOD Magazine CS Map-pack. They
will also be interviewed LIVE on CS Radio the day the map is released. If
you have any question or would like more information please contact our
Public Operations Manager: Matt Carson at Mattc@mod-mag.com
