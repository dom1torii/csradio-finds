Discussing: Voice Comm interview Back To CSR

{ Voice Comm interview }
posted Sunday, August 4, 2002 by u_suk_it[CSR]
The_Family_God, outkast, and I chatted with Valve's Erik Johnson for a few minutes the other day, and he answered a couple questions. I asked about the broadband codec that was rumored to be in development shortly after CS 1.3 was released. I thought people might like to know if it was in development and if so how far along the development was. Here's what was said:


ErikJ: Can you tell me anything about the broadband voice codec that was rumored to be in development for the voice comm system? Rumors have been floating around since the release of 1.3..


The broadband voice codec is more of a liscencing problem than a technical one.


But is it in development? Will it ever be released? Not even on the drawing board?


Right now Phillips owns the right for that codec, it's mainly used in Cell phones. We would need to have permission to redistribute from them to make it work. So far, we haven't had much luck talking to someone that can help us there.


Expensive liscencing fees perhaps too?


Haven't even got that far with them. There are some technical problems to solve also, since our voice traffic goes through the server and then down to the clients. We'd need to handle both codecs.


Oh another question about vcomm. Will it ever be possible to prevent certain people from hearing you over vcomm? Maybe like a private voice chat feature?


Not something we have planned right now.
