{ CSN "Not who you'd expect" Contest }
posted Saturday, August 17, 2002 by Some1 Call 911
CS-Nationhave started a contest! Here's a description of the contest and rules...

The premise of this contest is simple: send us a picture (or fifty) of someone that most people wouldn't think is a CS fan playing Counter-Strike.
For example, you could have an image of President Bush fragging terrorist scum on his computer screen. Or something.

The point is to be creative. The images can be doctored or actual pictures of someone (we wouldn't expect) playing CS. rules

» Your entry must arrive before 12 AM on August 28, 2002.
» It must be your image. You can't just steal other people's pictures/photoshops and pass it off as your own.
» Only send images in .jpg or .png format. No bitmaps, no zip files.
» No former or current CS-Nation team members. No former or current hosted site staff members.
You could win a playstation 2 or an XBox or 7 DVDs! Runner ups recieve a pre-order of Counter-Strike: Condition Zero, or a CS-Nationalist Upgrade (if you have an account over at CS-Nation). So get crackin' on those images and for more information head over to CS-Nation!
