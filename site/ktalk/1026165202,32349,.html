{ Counter-Life Beta 2 released }
posted Monday, July 8, 2002 by Some1 Call 911
Counter-Life Beta 2 was released earlier with some new changes.

  • M3 Super90 now works properly
  • Fixed Ammo issues(scout, G3SG1, M4A1, SG552)
  • Increase some of the weapons max carry ammo
  • Increase weapons damage (half of original damage in CS)
  • Increase HEGrenades damage
  • Increase max bank to $25000
  • Fixed sentry missing sound files
  • Flashbang no longer explodes in your hands
  • Shorter AK-47 reload time
  • Added High Poly w_knife
  • Increase each amount of money gained.
  • Fixed icons when healthkit is picked up
  • Fix various Model Issues

    If you don't know what Counter-Life is it replaces the Half-Life weapons with Counter-Strike weapons in single player. So, if you play Half-Life singleplayer and your a fan of Counter-Strike but want something different, try Counter-Life! If you have CS Retail your out of luck, Counter-Life won't work.
