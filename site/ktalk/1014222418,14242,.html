Recon 2000 DC 2002 Update!
Posted by: Jonathan Leckner at: 3:49:31 PM EST
First off we would like to give our gratitude for the many generous emails that we have received regarding our last event and the anticipation for our next event. Second we would like to apologize for the lack of any updates regarding Recon 2000 DC 2002. We have been working hard producing a large foundation for our company and are now seeking out possible locations for the next event. We are looking into a few different locations each being located around the DC Metropolitan area.

We will have additional news in the coming weeks where we will lay out our plans explaining just how we will be improving this event. We understand that organization and number of staff members needed was not to par last year and that is something we will be correcting. In the coming months we will be looking for many volunteers to form the staff needed to run an exciting, organized and successful event. If you wish to obtain additional information about volunteering please email me at jonathan@recon2000.com and I will add you to my volunteer list.

Once we have the date, times, and location secure we will release the information to you. Our first event, Recon 2000 DC 2001 was a major stepping-stone upon which became a good learning experience for us. From what we saw first hand, to the friendly emails containing suggestions on improving the event, there will be many changes at this year's event.

If you have any additional comments or suggestions for this years event please email suggestions@recon2000.com. We look forward to seeing you all this summer.
